An AI assistant that never
leaves your Mac.

Maple AI runs a capable language model directly on your Mac. Ask questions, draft text and read your documents with no account, no cloud and no internet needed after setup.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store

Requires a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later), macOS 14 or later, 16 GB of memory recommended and about 6 GB of free disk space.

Private by design

Your chats and files are processed on your Mac and stay there. Maple AI has no servers, no analytics and no tracking.

Works offline

After a one-time model download, Maple AI works on a plane, in a meeting room with no Wi-Fi, anywhere.

Reads your documents

Attach PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, RTF or text files and ask about them. Tables and slides come through intact.

Fast on Apple silicon

Built on Apple's MLX framework, replies stream at well over 100 tokens a second on a recent Mac.

Shows its thinking

See how Maple reasoned before it answered, or keep the thought process folded away.

Your chats, saved

Conversations are kept on your Mac so you can pick up where you left off, and delete them whenever you like.

About the model

Maple AI uses Maple, a 20-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts language model by DeepGrove AI, released under the MIT license. On first launch the app downloads it once (5.3 GB) from Hugging Face and checks every file. You can also point the app at a copy you already have.
