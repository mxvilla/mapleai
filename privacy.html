Privacy Policy

Effective 26 September 2026
In short: Maple AI does not collect, store or share any of your data. Everything you type or attach is processed on your Mac and stays on your Mac.

What the app processes

Your messages, the files you attach and Maple's replies are processed entirely by the language model running on your Mac. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

What is stored, and where

  • Chats are saved as files inside the app's private storage on your Mac so you can reopen them. You can delete any chat from the sidebar, or all of them by deleting the app.
  • Attached files are read only when you attach them. Maple AI keeps the extracted text as part of the chat it belongs to and does not keep a copy of the original file.
  • Settings (such as your standing instruction and file-size limit) are stored on your Mac.

Network use

Maple AI connects to the internet for one purpose only: downloading the language model from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) the first time you use the app, or if you delete and download it again. That download sends only a standard web request for the model files. Like any website, Hugging Face receives technical information such as your IP address, under Hugging Face's privacy policy. No chat content, files or personal information are included.

After the model is downloaded, Maple AI works without an internet connection.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • No sale or sharing of data, because we never have any.

Children

Maple AI does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

This website

This site is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies or analytics. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
