What do I need to run Maple AI?

A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) running macOS 14 or later, and about 6 GB of free disk space. 16 GB of memory or more is recommended; the model uses about 6 to 7 GB while running.

Why does it download 5.3 GB on first launch?

That is the language model itself. Maple AI downloads it once from Hugging Face, checks every file, and stores it inside the app. After that the app works offline. You can pause and resume the download. If you already have the model (deepgrove/maple-preview-2bit-mlx), choose "Use a Model Folder Already on This Mac" instead.

Which files can I attach?

PDF, Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), PowerPoint (.pptx), RTF, OpenDocument text, and plain text, Markdown, CSV and code files. Scanned PDFs without a text layer and images are not supported yet. For Pages, Numbers or Keynote documents, use File › Export To in that app and attach the exported file. Older .xls and .ppt files need to be saved as .xlsx or .pptx first.

A long document was cut short

By default Maple reads up to about 32,000 tokens (roughly 25,000 words) per file. You can raise this in Maple AI › Settings. Longer inputs take longer to read.

How do I free up disk space?

Open Maple AI › Settings › Model and choose "Delete Downloaded Model…". Your chats are kept. The model downloads again the next time you chat.

Where are my chats stored? How do I delete them?

On your Mac, inside the app's own storage. Right-click a chat in the sidebar and choose Delete. Deleting the app removes all chats.

Replies are slower than usual

Very long chats and large attachments slow replies down. Start a new chat (⌘N) for a new topic. Make sure "Faster replies (FlashHead)" is on in Settings.

Can Maple AI be wrong?

Yes. Like every language model, Maple can make mistakes or state things confidently that are not true. Check important facts.
